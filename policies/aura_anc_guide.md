# Aura ANC Headphones — Troubleshooting & Specs
- **Finishes:** Matte Obsidian, Dune White, Sage.
- **Box Contents:** Aura ANC Headphones, USB-C cable, 3.5mm cable, airplane dual-prong adapter, travel case.
- **Multipoint Pairing:** Hold Power/Bluetooth button 5s until LED flashes blue/white, pair Device 1, turn off Bluetooth on Device 1, hold 5s again to pair Device 2, then re-enable Bluetooth on Device 1.
- **ANC Static/Hiss:** Update firmware in Lumen Sound app (v2.4+). Persistent hiss with ANC off indicates a hardware mic fault covered by warranty.
