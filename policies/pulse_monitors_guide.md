# Pulse Studio Monitors (Pulse 4" & Pulse 5") — Setup & Troubleshooting
- **Sizing:** Pulse 4" (40W RMS) fits desks under 120 cm wide. Pulse 5" (70W RMS) is for larger desks and studio rooms.
- **Ground-Loop Hum:** Switch from unbalanced RCA cables to balanced 1/4-inch TRS cables or plug monitors into a separate wall outlet away from PC GPUs.
