# Lumen Audio — Warranty, Missing Parts & Returns
- **Returns:** 30 days from delivery for undamaged items in original packaging.
- **2-Year Warranty:** Covers manufacturing defects (mic hiss, battery failure, amplifier faults). Excludes physical drop damage and liquid spills.
- **Missing Box Accessories:** Ship a free replacement once the customer confirms their Order Number and Shipping Address.
- **Escalation Rules:** Escalate refunds over $150, physical damage with photo attachments, or chargeback/legal threats.
